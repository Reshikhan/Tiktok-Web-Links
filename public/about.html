About Me

By ༄ R E S H ! ! ! 🚫- · Updated October 2026

I'm ༄ R E S H ! ! ! 🚫- — known online as RESH (@rashidkhanreshii). I'm a Computer Science student from Peshawar, Pakistan, and a digital creator obsessed with one thing: making cinematic video accessible to anyone with a phone.

How it started

Like most creators, I started with nothing but a smartphone and free apps. I couldn't afford expensive cameras, paid courses, or premium editing software — so I learned to squeeze cinematic results out of the tools I already had. I experimented with the Blackmagic Camera app, free color grading LUTs, and mobile editing workflows until my phone footage started looking like it came from gear costing ten times more.

When I began sharing what I learned on TikTok, something unexpected happened: people wanted the knowledge, not just the results. Beginners from Pakistan and beyond started asking how I shot and graded my videos. That's when this site was born — a single place where all my tutorials, free downloads, and resources live, instead of being scattered across social media posts.

What I share

Everything on this site is practical and free. You'll find:

  • Editing tutorials — step-by-step guides for shooting and editing cinematic video on your phone, from beginner basics to color grading.
  • Free LUTs — my hand-made color grading presets for the Blackmagic/filmic look, free to download and use in your own projects.
  • Blackmagic Camera guides — how to use the free Blackmagic Camera app like a pro: manual exposure, shutter angle, focus tools, and flat profiles for grading.
  • Nature and 4K videos — aesthetic footage and visual inspiration for editors.

Why free?

I learned everything the hard way — through trial, error, and piecing together scattered information online. Good knowledge about mobile filmmaking shouldn't be locked behind paywalls, especially for creators in places where expensive gear and courses simply aren't an option. If my free resources save you the months of guessing it cost me, this site has done its job.

That said, creating takes time. If you want premium LUT packs or personal guidance, you'll find my contact links on the homepage. But the core library here — the tutorials, the free LUTs, the guides — will always be free.

Where to find me

I'm most active on TikTok (@rashidkhanreshii), where I post editing tutorials and cinematic videos. You can also reach me on Instagram or by email at rashidkhanreshii@gmail.com. For business or collaboration inquiries, email is the fastest way to reach me.

What's next

I'm currently studying Computer Science with a long-term goal of building software that helps young people in underprivileged areas of Pakistan learn STEM skills. Content creation and this website are part of that same mission: proving that world-class creative work doesn't require expensive tools — just knowledge, practice, and the willingness to share.
